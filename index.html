01  Content Strategy

Content Strategy

The Three Content Roles

Attention-Grabbing

6–45 sec

Stops the scroll and sparks curiosity. Fast hooks, teasers, and scroll-stopping visuals.

Persuasion

45–180 sec

Explains value, builds confidence, and moves people toward action. Education with clear next steps.

Anchor

2–5+ min

Serves as a lasting brand centerpiece on a website or core channel. Builds deep trust and emotional connection.

Video Length as a Strategic Signal

6–15 sec
Fast Hook
Ideal for ads, retargeting, urgency, or one clear message with a single action.
15–45 sec
Short Social Promo
Best for Reels, Shorts, TikTok, quick announcements, and service teasers.
60–120 sec
Explainer / Brand Promo
Useful when the viewer needs context to understand the offer before acting.
2–5 min+
Anchor & Authority
Built for websites, launches, books, webinars, tutorials, and warm audiences.

Framework for Success

A

Attention

Stops the scroll and sparks curiosity in the first 6 to 45 seconds.

P

Persuasion

Explains value, builds confidence, and moves people toward action.

◆

Anchor

Serves as a lasting brand centerpiece on a website or core channel that establishes the brand connection.

Simple Decision Rule

Attention

Go short for stopping, retargeting, bumpers, or fast promos — one clear message, simple clips.

Explanation

Best for explainers and education when the viewer needs context or a service overview.

Emotional Buy-In

Use an anchor asset that lives on a website or campaign hub for the long term.

Trust

Add education, testimonials, case studies, proof-based content, and brand emphasis.

◆

When in doubt, match content length to audience temperature — colder audiences need a faster hook, while warmer audiences can support more depth.

02  Format Library

Core Multimedia Format Reference

A comprehensive reference of where each format works best, the recommended length, and its primary purpose.

Short-Form Reel / Vertical Promo

Best length: 15–45 sec  ·  Best use: fast awareness, quick promos, tips, and personality-driven content.

Ultra-Short Ad / Bumper

Best length: 6–15 sec  ·  Best use: one message, one hook, one call-to-action.

Standard Social Promo Video

Best length: 15–30 sec  ·  Best use: everyday advertising and promotional campaigns.

Explainer Video

Best length: 60–120 sec  ·  Best use: services, offers, processes, apps, and how-it-works content.

Carousel / Document Post

Best length: 5–10 slides  ·  Best platforms: LinkedIn, Instagram, Facebook  ·  Best use: step-throughs, launches, and education.

Static Graphic & Infographic

Best platforms: Reels, TikTok, Shorts, blog  ·  Best use: services, offers, processes, and promotional content.

Cinematic / Anchor Trailer

Best length: 10 sec–5 min  ·  Best use: videos, launch promos, case studies, and flagship brand assets.

Case Study & Deep Guide

Best length: 30 sec–5 min  ·  Best use: testimonials, proof points, results, and authority content.

Email Promo & Audiogram

Best use: service offers, podcast reposts, social audio clips, and landing-page support assets.
